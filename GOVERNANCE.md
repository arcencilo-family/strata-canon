# Governance

## Stewardship

Frank Böhm is the initial project steward and controls which version is designated canonical by this repository. Canonical status is a project-maintenance designation, not a restriction on CC BY 4.0 adaptations.

## Change path

1. Open an issue containing a concrete defect, ambiguity, or tested opportunity.
2. Identify the load-bearing function and strongest counterargument.
3. Prepare a marked candidate against stable filenames on a development branch. Preserve the exact snapshot of every released edition.
4. Run proportionate adversarial review and behavioral checks, including negative controls.
5. Record the decision, superseded version, and integrity receipts.
6. Publish a new version only after steward authorization.

Consensus, model praise, lineage, and elegance may inform inquiry but do not determine acceptance. A repair may narrow or delete a function.

## Publication versions and preservation

Strata and BES are released independently in separate repositories. Strata's first public edition is 1.0.0 (2026-10-21). An edition number marks an approved publication baseline, not validation of alignment, safety, or behavioral benefit.

The Strata edition covers the complete canon set. Keep filenames stable and identify each released edition by a permanent Git tag, release notes, citation metadata, and manifest checksums. Retain the papers' inherited lineage revisions and persistent identifiers. Do not renumber historical documents merely to match the publication edition.

Retain existing Git history after disclosure review. If a future public export needs a fresh history, first move the complete prior repository and its history into the private archive and retain a provenance mapping. Do not erase development records. Preserve authorship, licenses, and provenance in any export.

Never rewrite released history, move a released tag, or replace a released asset in place. Publish corrections as a new version and retain superseded releases. Move superseded internal files and completed records into the archive.

BES follows its own software versioning policy and has first public version 0.1.0. Its release number does not have to match Strata's.

## Forks and adaptations

Forks are welcome under CC BY 4.0. Clearly identify modifications and do not present an adaptation as the stewarded canonical Strata release. Alternative governance and incompatible successors should use a distinguishing name or explicit fork label.
