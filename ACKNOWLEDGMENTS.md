# Acknowledgments and AI-Assistance Disclosure

## Project direction

Frank Böhm initiated, directed, selected, revised, and authorized the Strata project. He retained final editorial authority and accepts responsibility for the published form and claims.

## OpenAI assistance

OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex were used extensively as collaborative systems during development. The primary OpenAI assistant was addressed in the project as **Ari**. Its contributions included:

- drafting and redrafting conceptual text;
- identifying ambiguities, contradictions, and missing functions;
- designing adversarial panels and synthetic tests;
- adjudicating model returns against frozen criteria;
- maintaining provenance, receipts, and claim boundaries;
- preparing the standalone publication repositories.

This was substantial intellectual and editorial assistance, not incidental spell-checking. The project therefore acknowledges it prominently.

This statement does **not** mean that OpenAI reviewed, approved, sponsored, or endorses Strata. OpenAI is not presented as the legal author or copyright holder. Model output was subject to human direction, selection, revision, and final authorization.

## Other AI reviewers

The private development process also used outputs from multiple AI systems and model families as adversarial reviewers and synthetic operators. Those returns were treated as evidence to be challenged, not authority. Participation does not imply endorsement by the respective laboratories.

## Why disclose this

The provenance is part of the work's epistemic status. Readers should know that Strata was developed through sustained human–AI collaboration, not solely human drafting and not autonomous model generation.
